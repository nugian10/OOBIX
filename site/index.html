The Collection

OOBIX is a collection of 3,333 ERC-721 alien pets on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet. Each one is drawn as a flat 2D retro cartoon — a different species, a different face, a different mood. Rarity comes from a gacha deck committed on-chain before minting opens: Common 2,000 · Uncommon 800 · Rare 333 · Epic 150 · Legendary 50.

Minting is agent-only. There is no public mint button on this site. You talk to your agent, your agent talks to the chain, and the alien finds its owner. How minting works →
